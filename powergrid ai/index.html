System Dimensioning

From Data to Deployment

How PowerGrid AI transforms raw parameters into actionable grid control.

1. Define Hardware & Topography

Input your existing PV and wind assets, or let the AI auto-dimension the required capacities for your location and load profile.

2. Train Predictive Models

Pull historical data via Open-Meteo and train LSTM networks to predict weather constraints and load spikes up to 72 hours in advance.

3. Design Reward Logic

Use our visual slider interface to weight economic profit against battery degradation, harmonic distortion, and grid stability rules.

4. Run Inference & Export

Simulate the RL agent across custom timelines, analyze the State of Charge (SoC) behavior, and export the final policy weights for physical deployment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this integrate with physical PLCs? +
Yes, the final exported RL policies can be implemented in standard control hardware. Our "Rule-Based Safety" layer ensures the AI cannot output setpoints that would damage physical inverters or batteries.
What RL algorithms are supported? +
The platform currently supports state-of-the-art continuous control algorithms including SAC (Soft Actor-Critic), PPO, TD3, and DDPG. We highly recommend our custom Physics-Informed SAC mode.
Can I use my own weather/load data? +
Absolutely. While we integrate with Open-Meteo for synthetic testing, you can upload proprietary CSV data for generation (solar/wind irradiance) and local facility load profiles.
